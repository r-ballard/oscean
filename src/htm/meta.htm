About the wiki.

This personal wiki uses Oscean, a static site generator written in Uxntal for Uxn.

Articles are native HTML fragments. A small lexicon defines hierarchy, while the generator builds navigation and incoming links.

The blog uses explicitly authored Gregorian dates in YYYY-MM-DD format.

Generator source and the retained stylesheet derive from Oscean under its MIT license. This fork contains new placeholder article content and does not include the original author's media collection.
